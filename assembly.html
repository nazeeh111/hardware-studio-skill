Limits and coordinate convention

Up to 8 part sources, 128 total features and 16 explicitly placed instances. Each source uses the feature-solid format. Translation is in millimeters within ±500; rotations are degrees within ±360. The transform is translation × Z rotation × Y rotation × X rotation. Rotation uses the source origin, not its center. All generated positions must remain within the local runtime’s coordinate limits.

The geometry check covers nominal rigid solids and selected region probes. It does not solve joints, motion, constraints, fastener fit, manufacturing tolerances or physical assembly.
