Box origin is its minimum X/Y/Z corner. Cylinder origin is the center of its base; its axis points toward positive X, Y or Z. All dimensions are millimeters. Probes check a selected material or void region, not the whole part.


Supported fields and limits
1–32 ordered add/cut features, with box size [X,Y,Z] or cylinder radius, height and axis. Coordinates stay within ±500 mm; positive lengths range from 0.01 to 500 mm; final spans cannot exceed 500 mm. IDs must be unique. The resulting volume and each operation’s volume change must exceed 0.001 cubic millimeters; disconnected bodies are rejected. Up to 16 material/void probes are checked after building and after STEP readback.
This modeler supports axis-aligned feature solids. Sketch extrusion, fillets, threads, lofts and mechanical assemblies are not supported. The feature JSON retains the operation sequence; STEP carries the resulting surfaces.
